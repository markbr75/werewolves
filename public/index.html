Werewolf

A village, a few secret werewolves, and one question: who is lying? Cameras on. When night falls every screen goes dark, and only the wolves can see and hear each other.

or join one

4 to 18 players. Everyone joins from their own phone or laptop.

This is a private, invite-only game. Video and sound are live only: nothing is recorded or stored.

How a game goes

  1. Gather. Everyone joins with their camera on and is dealt a secret role.
  2. Night. The village sleeps and screens go black. The werewolves wake, see only each other, and choose a victim.
  3. Dawn. The village learns who did not survive.
  4. Day. Cameras back on. Argue, accuse, defend yourself, then vote to banish one player.
  5. Repeat until every wolf is banished, or the wolves match the villagers in number.

Who you might be

  • WerewolfKills one villager each night. Hides in plain sight by day.
  • SeerEach night learns whether one player is a werewolf.
  • DoctorEach night protects one player from the wolves.
  • WitchOne potion to save a life, one to take a life. Each works once.
  • HunterFires one last shot at anyone when they die.
  • VillagerNo powers. Just eyes, ears and a vote.
